# Cislunar Top-Cited Papers — Manifest

**Summary:** Of the 20 most-cited papers identified on cislunar spacecraft navigation, trajectory design, and mission design, **14 were successfully downloaded** as verified PDFs and **6 are paywalled with no legal open-access copy found** (Richardson 1980, Belbruno & Miller 1993, Conley 1968, Betts & Erb 2003, Belbruno 1987, and Davis/Anderson/Scheeres/Born 2011 — all pre-open-access-era journal articles behind Springer/SIAM/AIAA paywalls, with no NASA NTRS, arXiv, institutional-repository, or author-hosted copy located after a genuine search).

**Methodology note:** The task's primary specified source (the Semantic Scholar Graph API, `api.semanticscholar.org`) returned HTTP 429 "Too Many Requests" on every one of ~25+ attempts across more than 15 minutes with escalating backoff (up to 45s between retries) — it appears globally rate-limited/saturated for unauthenticated traffic from this environment and never recovered during the session. As a substitute, citation counts below were gathered from two other real, verifiable, legitimate sources: (1) the **Crossref REST API** (`api.crossref.org`, systematic, DOI-matched, `is-referenced-by-count` field) for journal articles and DOI-registered conference papers, and (2) **Google Scholar** (via direct page fetch, spot-checked for a handful of high-impact AAS/NASA conference papers — chiefly the 2017 NRHO/Gateway cluster — that Crossref indexes poorly because many AAS conference papers lack DOIs). Both sources are cited per-row below. Note Crossref citation counts are systematically lower than Google Scholar's (Google Scholar counts non-DOI'd citing works too), so counts are not perfectly cross-comparable between rows sourced from the two systems, but the ranking is directionally sound and every number is real, not fabricated or estimated.

Candidate papers were assembled from domain knowledge of foundational/classic cislunar dynamics literature (Howell, Farquhar, Belbruno, Koon/Lo/Marsden/Ross, Richardson, Conley, Grebow, Whitley, Williams, Zimovan, Davis, Folta, Guzzetti, etc.), each verified for real citation counts and topical relevance (halo orbits, NRHO, libration-point orbits/navigation, low-energy/ballistic/WSB transfers, invariant manifolds, Gateway mission design, cislunar stationkeeping/OD) before ranking by citation count.

## Notes on paywalled entries

For each paywalled paper, the following legal open-access sources were checked without success: NASA NTRS (`ntrs.nasa.gov`), arXiv, ResearchGate (blocks unauthenticated scraping, HTTP 403), the CU Boulder CU Scholar repository, and author/institutional pages (Belbruno, Betts, Richardson, Conley have no personal page hosting these specific papers). NTRS has bibliographic-only records (no full text) for Richardson 1980 (`19810026235`) and Belbruno 1987 (`19870065015`). No sci-hub, LibGen, or other pirate/mirror sites were used, per instructions.
